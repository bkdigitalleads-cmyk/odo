Odo Support

Odo: Teen Driving Log is a supervised-driving practice log for iPhone. Log every practice drive, track your progress against your state's required hours, and export an official-style PDF log for your DMV visit.

Frequently asked questions

How do I log a drive?

Tap the ＋ button on the home screen. Start the timer when you pull out and stop it when you park, or just type the minutes in directly. Use the night chips to record how much of the drive was after dark.

How do I change my state or goal?

Settings → State. Pick your state to load its requirement, or choose a custom goal if you want to aim higher (or your state changes its rules).

Can I add hours I drove before Odo?

Yes. Enter your starting day and night hours when you set up the app, or any time in Settings → Hours before Odo. They count toward your goal and print on the PDF as their own line, "Hours logged before Odo (entered by parent)", separate from your dated drives.

How do I get the PDF log?

Open the Log tab and tap Create PDF. The PDF includes dated entries, day and night columns, totals, and parent or guardian signature lines. The PDF and CSV export are part of Odo Pro; on the free plan, tap Preview my PDF to see a sample copy first.

What does the free plan include?

New installs can log 2 drives for free. Odo Pro unlocks unlimited drives, the PDF and CSV export, and Face ID lock. The Permit Pass is a one-time purchase that covers the whole permit and is yours forever.

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple Account you bought Odo Pro with.

Does Odo work without a signal?

Yes. Odo works offline, anywhere you drive, and your log is saved on your iPhone. See the privacy policy for details.

Are the state requirements official?

State practice-hour requirements are provided as a convenience and verified against public sources, but rules change, so always confirm current requirements and required forms with your state's DMV before your road test.
