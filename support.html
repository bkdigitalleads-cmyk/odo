Odo — Support

Odo: Teen Driving Log is a supervised-driving practice log for iPhone. Log every practice drive, track your progress against your state's required hours, and export an official-style PDF log for your DMV visit.

Frequently asked questions

How do I log a drive?

Tap the ＋ button on the home screen. Start the timer when you pull out and stop it when you park — or just type the minutes in directly. Use the night chips to record how much of the drive was after dark.

How do I change my state or goal?

Settings → State. Pick your state to load its requirement, or choose a custom goal if you want to aim higher (or your state changes its rules).

How do I get the PDF log?

Tap Export on the home screen (or Settings → Export). The PDF includes dated entries, day/night columns, totals, and parent/guardian signature lines. PDF export is part of Odo Pro.

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple Account you bought Odo Pro with.

Is my data private?

Yes — your entire log lives on your phone. No account, no cloud, no GPS tracking. See the privacy policy.

Are the state requirements official?

State practice-hour requirements are provided as a convenience and verified against public sources, but rules change — always confirm current requirements and required forms with your state's DMV before your road test.
