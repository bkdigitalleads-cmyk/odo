## Description (1.0.6 draft, paste into ASC with the 1.0.6 submission)
Odo is the teen driving hours log that gets your family to the road test with the paperwork done.

Most states want a set number of supervised practice hours before a teen can take the road test, often 50 or more, with some of them at night. Odo keeps count for you. Pick your state and the goal sets itself, log each drive in seconds, and watch two rings fill up: total hours on the outside, night hours on the inside. When it's time, hand the DMV a clean, signed log.

WALK INTO THE DMV WITH THE LOG DONE
• One tap turns your drives into an official-style PDF with dated entries, day and night columns, totals, and parent or guardian signature lines
• Print it, AirDrop it, or email it to the driving school
• CSV export for your own records

ALWAYS KNOW HOW MANY HOURS ARE LEFT
• Practice-hour requirements built in for all 50 states and DC
• Night hours get their own ring, matched to your state's rule
• Set a custom goal if your state changes its rules or you want to aim higher

ALREADY STARTED? BRING YOUR HOURS
• Kept a paper log or used another app? Enter your starting day and night hours when you set up Odo
• They count toward your goal and show on the PDF as their own clearly labeled line, separate from your dated drives
• Change them any time in Settings

LOG A DRIVE BEFORE YOU'RE OUT OF THE CAR
• Start the timer when you pull out and stop it when you park, or type the minutes in later
• Weather, road types, supervising adult and notes on every drive
• Works offline, anywhere you drive
• Optional Face ID lock

THE PERMIT PASS
Your first 2 drives are free, and you can preview your DMV log before you pay for anything. Odo Pro unlocks unlimited drives, the PDF log, CSV export and Face ID lock. The Permit Pass is a one-time purchase that covers the whole permit and is yours forever. Subscriptions are also available.

Odo is a logging tool. State requirements are provided as a convenience, so always confirm current rules and required forms with your state's DMV.

## App Review note for 1.0.6 (paste into ASC, Notes)
Odo is an offline log of supervised practice drives for teens with a learner's permit. There is no account or sign-in.

On first launch you see three short intro screens, pick a state, say whether you already logged some hours, and answer one question about where you heard of Odo. If the Apple ID can still get the free trial, two screens explain the trial and the reminder. The reminder screen asks for notification permission, which is only used for one local reminder 1 day before the trial ends. Then the paywall opens. The close button (X, top right) appears after 3 seconds. Tap it to keep using the app for free.

The app works free up to the drive limit (2 drives on a new install). After that, tapping + opens the paywall. The PDF and CSV exports are part of Pro. Free users can open a preview of the PDF from the Log tab; it is marked SAMPLE, and its Share and Print buttons open the paywall. Restore purchases is on the paywall and in Settings.

As set in App Store Connect, Oct 20 2026:

- From 1.0.7 the app hides Yearly on both paywalls, because it costs the same as the one-time Permit Pass. It stays in App Store Connect and RevenueCat, and anyone who already bought Yearly keeps Pro and can still restore it.

RevenueCat: entitlement `pro`, offering `default` with all three packages (confirmed Oct 20): $rc_weekly → odo_pro_weekly, $rc_annual → odo_pro_yearly, $rc_lifetime → odo_pro_lifetime.

App code needs no change for these: prices and trial lengths are read from StoreKit, and trial wording only shows when RevenueCat reports the Apple ID as eligible for a free intro offer on that product, so Yearly now shows its plain price everywhere.
