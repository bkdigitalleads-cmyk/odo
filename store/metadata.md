## Description
Odo turns the crumpled paper driving log into something a teen will actually keep — and a parent will actually trust.

Every state that licenses teen drivers expects supervised practice, and most require an exact number of hours — often 50 or more, with some at night. Odo tracks it all: pick your state and the goal sets itself, log each drive in seconds, and watch the progress bars fill until the license is in reach.

LOG A DRIVE IN SECONDS
• Tap start when you pull out, stop when you park — or type the minutes in later
• Day and night time tracked separately, matched to your state's rule
• Weather, road types, supervising adult and notes on every entry

BUILT AROUND YOUR STATE
• Practice-hour requirements preloaded for all 50 states + DC
• Night-hours requirement tracked on its own progress bar
• Custom goal mode if your state changes its rules or you want to aim higher

A LOG THE DMV WILL LOVE
• One tap creates an official-style PDF: dated entries, day/night columns, totals, and parent/guardian signature lines
• CSV export for your own records
• Print it, AirDrop it, or email it to the driving school

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account. No cloud. No GPS tracking of your teen.
• The entire log lives on the phone — export it whenever you like
• Optional Face ID lock

FREE TO START
Log your first 10 drives free. Odo Pro unlocks unlimited drives, the PDF log, CSV export, and Face ID lock — as a single one-time purchase (yours forever) or a low-cost yearly subscription.

Odo is a logging tool: state requirements are provided as a convenience — always confirm current rules and required forms with your state's DMV.
