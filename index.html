Frequently asked questions

How many supervised driving hours does my state require?
Most states require 40–60 supervised hours before the road test, usually with 10 or more at night. Odo ships with the requirement for every state and DC, so your progress bar is measuring the right target from day one.

How do I prove my practice hours to the DMV?
Many states ask for a signed log when you schedule the road test. Odo's PDF export lists every drive by date, duration, day/night, and conditions, with totals and supervisor signature lines — print it or email it straight from the app.

Do night hours count separately?
Yes — and Odo gives them their own progress bar so you're never surprised at the end.

Is it free?
Your first 10 drives are free. Odo Pro (one-time purchase or yearly) unlocks unlimited drives and PDF/CSV export.
