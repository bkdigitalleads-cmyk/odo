Frequently asked questions

How many supervised driving hours does my state require?
Most states require 40 to 60 supervised hours before the road test, usually with 10 or more at night. Odo ships with the requirement for every state and DC, so your progress is measured against the right target from day one.

How do I prove my practice hours to the DMV?
Many states ask for a signed log when you schedule the road test. Odo's PDF export lists every drive by date, duration, day/night, and conditions, with totals and supervisor signature lines. Print it or email it straight from the app.

Do night hours count separately?
Yes, and Odo gives them their own progress ring so you're never surprised at the end.

Is it free?
Your first 2 drives are free, and you can preview your DMV log before you pay. Odo Pro unlocks unlimited drives and the PDF and CSV export. The Permit Pass is a one-time purchase that covers the whole permit and is yours forever. A weekly plan is also available.
