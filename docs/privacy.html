Swirl Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026

Swirl is built so that your wine tasting journal is nobody's business but yours.

What we collect: almost nothing

Your wines, ratings, tasting notes, label photos, producers, regions, prices, and everything else you log are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded, synced, or transmitted anywhere by Swirl. There are no accounts and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Purchases

If you buy Swirl Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat solely to validate purchases and restore them; RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and purchase history, never your journal data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

Camera & photos

Camera and photo-library access are used only to attach photos of wine labels to your tasting entries. Images are stored on-device only.

Data deletion

Settings → Delete all wines & photos erases everything instantly. Deleting the app does the same.
