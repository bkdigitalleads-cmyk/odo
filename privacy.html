Odo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Odo ("the app") is a supervised-driving practice log for iPhone, made by Brian Kershner. It is designed so that your data stays on your device.

What we collect: almost nothing

Your driving log — drives, dates, durations, supervisor names, notes, and any names you enter — is stored only in the app's local database on your iPhone. It is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to us or anyone else. The app works fully offline and has no account system. Odo does not use GPS and does not track anyone's location.

Purchases

If you buy Odo Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to validate purchases. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous identifier and your purchase history for this app — never your name, contacts, or driving log. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Analytics and tracking

Odo contains no analytics SDKs, no ads, and no tracking. We do not collect usage data.

Exports

PDF and CSV exports are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them (print, email, AirDrop, etc.).

Face ID

If you enable the app lock, authentication is handled entirely by iOS. Odo never sees your biometric data.

Children

Odo is intended for teen drivers and their parents. Because no personal data leaves the device, no personal data about anyone — including minors — is collected by us.

Deleting your data

Delete any drive in the app, use Settings → Delete all drives, or simply delete the app — everything it stored goes with it.
